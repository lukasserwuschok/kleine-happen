Privacy Policy

for the Android app “Little Bites” (German: “Kleine Happen”) · Last updated: October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

In short: Little Bites does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. The app does not transmit anything to me or to third parties.

2. What data the app processes

In the app you can record, for example, meals, milk feeds, nappies, foods tried, allergens introduced, milestones, weekly plans, a shopping list, your own recipes and, optionally, your child's first name and date of birth.

This information is stored exclusively in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transferred to any server. As the developer, I have no access to it.

3. No transfer, no tracking

  • The app does not connect to my own or any third-party servers. Fonts and all content are included in the app.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking services are used.
  • No cookies are used and no device identifiers are read.

The “internet access” permission is technically required because the app's interface is displayed in a built-in web view. It is not used to transfer data.

4. Backup and export

With “Share backup” and “Share as spreadsheet” you can export your data as a file. The file only leaves your device if you pass it on yourself via the Android share menu, for example by email or messenger. The service you choose is then responsible for any further processing.

If you have enabled device backup to your Google account in your Android settings, Android may include the app data in that backup. This backup is managed by Google as part of your account. I have no access to it.

5a. Web version for iPhone (GitHub Pages)

The web version at lukasserwuschok.github.io/kleine-happen/ is served via GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address to deliver the files and secure the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information: GitHub privacy statement. In the web version too, your entries are stored only in the browser storage on your device and are not transmitted. If you clear website data in Safari, your entries are deleted as well.

5. Google Play

When you download the app via Google Play, Google processes data under its own terms, for example for purchase, installation and updates. I have no influence on this. You can find more information in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Contact by email

If you send me an email, I process your address and the content only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Emails are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed.

7. Deletion

You can remove all data at any time via “Settings → Delete all data”. When you uninstall the app, Android deletes all app data automatically.

8. Your rights

Since I do not receive any personal data through the app, I cannot provide information about app data. For email enquiries you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Germany) or the authority in your country of residence.

9. Children

The app is intended for parents and carers, not for children. Information about the child is entered voluntarily by you and stays on your device.

10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that data is processed differently, this policy will be updated beforehand.

In case of doubt, the German version applies.
